MeteorShield Sarawak

NASA Space Apps Challenge, October 2025

View

A warning and risk visualisation tool for asteroids and earthquakes that could affect Sarawak and Southeast Asia. It pulls asteroid data from NASA's NEO Feed API and earthquake data from USGS, and shows both on hazard maps.

You can simulate an impact (blast radius, crater, tsunami and seismic effects), and it uses an LLM to write a short summary and a PDF report for emergency planning. There is also a mode where you click any spot on the map and launch an asteroid at it, to help people understand planetary defence.

Built with Python, Streamlit, Matplotlib and Plotly.
